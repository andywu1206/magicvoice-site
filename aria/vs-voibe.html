Aria vs Voibe

Updated 15 October 2026 · 3 min read

Aria Magic Voice and Voibe are on the same side of the most important question: both keep your audio on your Mac instead of uploading it. The real difference is what the app is. Aria is a full input method — dictation and typing in one surface, with code-switching and direct text injection. If you want a focused dictation tool with a simpler surface, Voibe is a reasonable pick.

The honest framing

This page exists because Voibe's editorial roundups come up constantly for offline dictation on Mac — and those lists are genuinely useful. Rather than pretend otherwise: the comparison that matters for you is not "which app is better", it is which app matches how you write.

Where Aria is the better fit

  • Your documents mix languages and you switch mid-sentence without thinking about it.
  • You dictate into many apps, and a copy-paste step per app is friction you notice.
  • You want one tool for typing and talking, not two separate behaviours.
  • You want your jargon to get more accurate over time without maintaining a word list.

Where a focused dictation app is the better fit

  • You dictate in one language and mostly into one app.
  • You want a small, single-purpose surface and nothing else on screen.
  • You already have a typing setup you like and just want speech added.

All three are good reasons. Install the one that fits; they are not mutually exclusive, and you can run Aria alongside another tool.

One test that settles it

Dictate the paragraph you actually write every day — same text, both apps, Wi-Fi off. Then count corrections. The tool that wins on your text is the one to keep. Background on the offline test: the short answer.
