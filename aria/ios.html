它能做什么

  • 语音输入点一下说话，语音在手机本机转成文字，边说边出现在输入框里。

怎么开始用

  1. 在 App Store 下载并安装 Aria 输入法。
  2. 打开「设置 › 通用 › 键盘 › 键盘 › 添加新键盘…」，选择 Aria输入法。

常见问题

为什么要打开「允许完全访问」？

只用来让键盘和主 App 在手机本机共享词库与设置。Aria 键盘不会借它联网，也不会读取或上传你输入的内容。

我的语音会被上传吗？

不会。点一下说话时才会用到麦克风，语音在手机本机转成文字，不存储，也不外发。

Get started

  2. Open Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard…, then choose Aria输入法.
  3. In the same list, tap Aria输入法 and turn on Allow Full Access.

Questions

Why does it ask for Allow Full Access?

It is only used to share your vocabulary and settings between the keyboard and the app on your phone. The Aria keyboard does not use it to go online, and it does not read or upload what you type.

Is my voice uploaded?

No. The microphone is used only when you tap to talk, speech is transcribed on the phone, and nothing is stored or sent out.

At Harry App Store.

It is a paid download on the App Store. The price is shown on the App Store page.
