Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Overview

Aria Magic Voice is a macOS desktop application for local AI-powered speech-to-text conversion. We take your privacy seriously and have designed the product with a privacy-first approach from day one.

In short: your voice data never leaves your computer.

Voice and Text Data

  • All speech recognition is performed 100% locally on your Mac using the SenseVoice AI engine.
  • No audio or text data is ever uploaded to any cloud server.
  • No third-party cloud speech recognition service is used.
  • Transcribed text exists only in your device's local memory and is released when no longer needed.

Local Storage

Application configuration is stored locally at:

~/.config/magicvoice/

This directory contains only app settings (hotkey preferences, language selection, etc.) and does not contain any voice or text data.

Network Connections

Aria Magic Voice does not require an internet connection for normal use. The only network requests occur during:

  • License activation and verification — When you activate or verify your license key, the app connects to our payment provider (Paddle) to validate the license. Only the license key is transmitted; no voice, text, or personal data is involved.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No personal identifiable information
  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • No cookies
  • No account registration required
  • No usage behavior or frequency logging
  • No device information or system logs

Payment Processing

Purchases are processed by Paddle.com, which acts as our Merchant of Record. When you make a purchase, Paddle collects payment information (such as your name, email address, and payment details) directly. We do not have access to your full payment information. Paddle's handling of your data is governed by their Privacy Policy.

This Website

ariamagicvoice.com is a purely static website hosted on GitHub Pages. This website does not use any analytics tools, trackers, or cookies.

Third-Party Services

Apart from Paddle for payment processing and license verification, Aria Magic Voice does not integrate any third-party services. Speech recognition runs entirely locally and does not depend on any external API.

Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, there are no concerns regarding children's data collection. Users of any age may safely use the product.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the content on this page and revise the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

magicvoice.team@outlook.com
