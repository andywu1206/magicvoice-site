Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Overview

Aria Magic Voice is a macOS desktop application for local AI-powered speech-to-text conversion. We take your privacy seriously and have designed the product with a privacy-first approach from day one.

In short: your voice data never leaves your computer.

Voice and Text Data

  • All speech recognition is performed 100% locally on your Mac using the SenseVoice AI engine.
  • No audio or text data is ever uploaded to any cloud server.
  • No third-party cloud speech recognition service is used.
  • Transcribed text exists only in your device's local memory and is released when no longer needed.

Local Storage

Application configuration is stored locally at:

~/.config/magicvoice/

This directory contains only app settings (hotkey preferences, language selection, etc.) and does not contain any voice or text data.

Network Connections

Aria Magic Voice does not require an internet connection for any functionality, including license activation. All speech recognition and license verification are performed entirely on your device.

  • License activation — License keys are validated offline using cryptographic signatures. The app never contacts any server to activate or verify your license.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No personal identifiable information
  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • No cookies
  • No account registration required
  • No usage behavior or frequency logging
  • No device information or system logs

Payment Processing

Purchases are processed by Paddle.com, which acts as our Merchant of Record. When you make a purchase, Paddle collects payment information (such as your name, email address, and payment details) directly. We do not have access to your full payment information. Paddle's handling of your data is governed by their Privacy Policy.

This Website

ariamagicvoice.com is a purely static website hosted on GitHub Pages. This website does not use any analytics tools, trackers, or cookies. However, the website loads resources from the following external domains in order to render its pages:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) — for web typography. Google may log standard access data; see Google Fonts Privacy FAQ.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN (cdn.tailwindcss.com) — for page styling.
  • Phosphor Icons (unpkg.com) — for UI icons.
  • Flag CDN (flagcdn.com) — for country flag images.
  • Paddle.js (paddle.com) — for in-page checkout functionality.

When your browser loads these resources, your IP address and basic request headers are sent to the respective CDN providers as part of standard HTTP requests. We do not control data handling by these providers.

Third-Party Services

The desktop application does not integrate any third-party cloud services. Speech recognition runs entirely locally and does not depend on any external API. License keys are validated offline using cryptographic signatures — the app never contacts any server for activation or verification.

This website loads third-party resources from Google Fonts, Tailwind CSS CDN, unpkg (Phosphor Icons), Flag CDN, and Paddle as described in the "This Website" section above. These services may receive standard HTTP request data (such as your IP address) when the page loads.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • App data — All application data (settings, preferences) is stored locally on your Mac. You can delete all app data by removing the application and its configuration directory at ~/.config/magicvoice/.
  • Payment data — Payment and transaction information is handled by Paddle and is subject to Paddle's privacy and data retention policy.
  • Data deletion requests — If you would like to request deletion of any data associated with your purchase or have questions about your data, please contact us at magicvoice.team@outlook.com.

Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, there are no concerns regarding children's data collection. Users of any age may safely use the product.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the content on this page and revise the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

magicvoice.team@outlook.com
