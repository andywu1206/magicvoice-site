Permissions

How to grant Accessibility permission:

  1. Open System Settings

  3. Click the + button and add MagicVoice

Troubleshooting

  • No sound / not recognizing — Check that Microphone permission is granted. Make sure the correct microphone is selected in your system Sound settings.
  • Text not inserting — Check that Accessibility permission is granted. Confirm MagicVoice is listed under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  • Hotkey conflict — Try changing to a different hotkey combination in Settings.
