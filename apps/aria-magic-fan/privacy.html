Privacy Policy · Aria Magic Fan

Effective date: 2026-10-01　·　Applies to the Aria Magic Fan application for macOS

Aria Magic Fan is designed with a strict local-first and privacy-by-default architecture. We believe utility tools should do their job without tracking you.

Zero Network Collection

Aria Magic Fan contains no network tracking code, analytics SDKs, telemetry, or remote servers. All hardware sensor measurements and fan control logic execute exclusively on your local Mac.

Hardware Data & System Permissions

The application reads system thermal sensors and fan speeds solely for display and automated cooling curve calculation. This data is never written to disk permanently and never transmitted over the internet.

License & Activation

Purchases and offline license verifications use cryptographic asymmetric signature verification against local hardware hashes. No personal identifiers or browsing data are ever collected or stored.

Contact

Questions about our privacy policy can be directed to: magicvoice.team@outlook.com.

隐私政策 · Aria魔法散热风扇

生效日期：2026-10-01　·　适用于 macOS 平台的 Aria Magic Fan（Aria魔法散热风扇）

Aria Magic Fan 采用严格的本地优先与默认隐私保护架构。我们深信，系统工具软件应当纯粹高效地完成其使命，绝不刺探或收集用户的任何个人信息。

零网络请求与零遥测

Aria Magic Fan 不包含任何第三方数据统计、广告追踪 SDK 或远程遥测服务。所有的温度传感器采样、风扇目标转速运算均仅在您的 Mac 本地内存中完成。

硬件数据与系统权限

软件请求的系统特权仅用于与本地 SMC（系统管理控制器）通信以下发散热转速。温度与转速数据绝不持久化存储，更不会上传至任何云端服务器。

授权与激活安全

商业版许可证采用离线非对称密码学算法进行本地校验，不依赖云端验证服务器，不采集您的账号、密码或设备敏感个人身份信息。
