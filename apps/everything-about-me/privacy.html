隐私政策 · i人洞府

生效日期：2026-09-13　·　本政策适用于 i人洞府（Everything About Me）macOS / iOS App

「i人洞府」把个人资料与重要日期留在你自己的设备上。我们把对隐私的尊重放在第一位。

1. 我们不收集任何个人数据

不设账号、不要求注册；不收集姓名、邮箱、电话或任何身份信息。不使用任何分析、追踪、广告或第三方统计，不记录你的使用行为。

除两件事外，应用本身不联网：一是 App 内购买由 Apple 处理；二是你主动点开的官方链接会在浏览器中打开。你的资料不会因此离开设备。

2. 资料与照片如何处理

  • 导入的照片、PDF 与文字资料只保存在你的设备本地。
  • 文字识别（OCR）在本机完成，不上传。
  • 你可以自行导出或恢复备份，备份文件由你保管。

3. 提醒与通知

到期提醒由设备本地计算并发出本地通知，不经过我们的服务器。

4. 推广与购买

免费版会在应用内显示一条产品推广。可选的一次买断（Pro）仅移除这条推广，其余功能完全一致。购买由 Apple 处理，我们不接触也不保存你的支付信息。

5. 数据存储与删除

所有数据保存在你设备的本地存储中，由你完全掌控。删除应用或清除数据后我们没有任何副本——因为我们从未持有。

6. 不与任何人共享

因为我们不收集数据，所以没有任何数据可被共享、出售或转移。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人（含儿童）的个人信息。

8. 变更与联系

若未来推出需要联网或账号的功能，会在启用前更新本政策并明确告知。隐私相关问题请联系：magicvoice.team@outlook.com

Privacy Policy · Everything About Me

Effective date: 2026-09-13　·　This policy applies to the Everything About Me (i人洞府) macOS / iOS app

Everything About Me keeps your records and important dates on your own device. We put your privacy first.

1. We collect no personal data

No account, no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email, phone number or any identifying information. There is no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDK, and we do not log your activity.

Apart from two things, the app itself does not use the network: in-app purchases are handled by Apple, and official links you choose to open are opened in your browser. Your records do not leave your device because of either.

2. How documents and photos are handled

  • Imported photos, PDFs and text are stored only on your device.
  • Text recognition (OCR) runs on-device; nothing is uploaded.
  • You can export or restore your own backups; the backup files stay with you.

3. Reminders and notifications

Deadline reminders are computed on your device and delivered as local notifications. They do not pass through our servers.

4. Promotions and purchases

The free version shows a single in-app promotion. The optional one-time Pro purchase removes that promotion only; every other feature is identical. Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

5. Storage and deletion

All data lives in local storage on your device and stays under your control. After you delete the app or clear its data we hold no copy, because we never had one.

6. We share nothing

Because we collect no data, there is no data to share, sell or transfer.

7. Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes and contact

If a future version adds cloud or account features, we will update this policy and tell you before those features are enabled. Privacy questions: magicvoice.team@outlook.com
