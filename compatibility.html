Compatibility

System requirements and supported configurations

Last updated: March 30, 2026

System Requirements

Operating System macOS 13.0 (Ventura) or later
Processor Apple Silicon (M1 / M2 / M3 / M4) or Intel x86_64
Memory (RAM) 4 GB minimum, 8 GB recommended
Disk Space ~500 MB (includes AI model files)
Microphone Built-in or external microphone
Internet Not required

Supported Mac Models

Apple Silicon

Recommended
  • MacBook Air (M1, 2020 and later)
  • MacBook Pro (M1, 2020 and later)
  • Mac mini (M1, 2020 and later)
  • iMac (M1, 2021 and later)
  • Mac Studio (M1 Max, 2022 and later)
  • Mac Pro (M2 Ultra, 2023 and later)

Intel

Supported
  • MacBook Air (2018 and later)
  • MacBook Pro (2018 and later)
  • Mac mini (2018 and later)
  • iMac (2017 and later)
  • iMac Pro (2017)
  • Mac Pro (2019)

* Intel Macs must be running macOS 13.0 Ventura or later. Apple Silicon Macs deliver faster recognition speed due to the Neural Engine.

Required macOS Permissions

Aria Magic Voice requires the following permissions to function properly. You will be prompted to grant these on first launch.

Microphone Access

Required to capture your voice for speech recognition. All audio is processed locally and never stored or transmitted.

Accessibility

Required for the double-tap hotkey detection and automatic text insertion at the cursor position.

Input Monitoring

Required for detecting the double-tap hotkey (Control, Option, or Command key) to activate voice input.
