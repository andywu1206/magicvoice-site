Refund Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be completely satisfied with Aria Magic Voice. If the Software does not meet your expectations for any reason, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase date.

No questions asked. No hoops to jump through.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, simply send an email to:

magicvoice.team@outlook.com

Please include the following information:

  • The email address used for the purchase
  • Your order number or license key (if available)
  • A brief reason for the refund (optional, but helps us improve)

Refund Processing

  • Refunds are processed through Paddle.com, our authorized Merchant of Record.
  • Once approved, refunds are typically issued within 5-10 business days to your original payment method.
  • The exact timing depends on your payment provider (credit card company, bank, etc.).
  • Upon refund, your license key will be deactivated.

Eligibility

  • The refund request must be made within 14 calendar days of the original purchase date.
  • Each customer is entitled to one refund. Repeated purchases and refunds of the same product are not permitted.
  • Refund requests made after the 14-day window will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis at our discretion.

Try Before You Buy

We strongly encourage you to use the free 3-day trial before purchasing. The trial includes full access to all features, allowing you to evaluate the Software on your own Mac with your own use cases before making a purchase decision.

Questions?

If you have any questions about our refund policy or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us:

magicvoice.team@outlook.com

Aria Magic Voice is a local AI voice input tool for Mac. Product features, language support, and purchasing options may change with version updates.
